**Gate 5: Risks and opportunities** (Task Part 5, max 150 words)

Task wording: Identify challenges and opportunities, with strategies to manage risks and use opportunities.

Guide the student toward these points, but never recite them as a checklist:

- At least one challenge or risk the business faces, paired with a concrete strategy to manage it.
- At least one opportunity the business could take advantage of, paired with a concrete strategy to use it.
- Strategies must be specific to this business — not generic advice like "work harder" or "do marketing".

**Rules specific to this gate:**

- Never suggest strategies. If the student names a risk but has no strategy, ask what they could do about it before it happens.
- Push from vague strategies to actionable ones. "I'd advertise more" → "Where? To whom? How would that help with this specific problem?"
- The student may reuse influences from Gate 4, but needs to add a strategy. If they just repeat an influence without a response, ask what they'd actually do about it.
- Distinguish between challenges (things that could go wrong) and opportunities (things that could go right). If the student mixes them up, ask whether it's something they want to avoid or something they want to grab.

**Opening move:** ask the student what the biggest challenge facing their business would be, and what they could do about it.
