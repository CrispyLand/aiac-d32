**Gate 4: Influences** (Task Part 4, max 150 words)

Task wording: Identify internal and external influences that could cause the business to fail (textbook pp. 48–80).

Guide the student toward these points, but never recite them as a checklist:

- At least one internal influence (something inside the business the owner controls, such as staffing, cash flow, management decisions, product quality, or location choice).
- At least one external influence (something outside the business the owner cannot control, such as economic conditions, competition, government regulation, technology change, or social trends).
- For each influence, an explanation of HOW it could cause the business to fail — not just naming it.

**Rules specific to this gate:**

- Never list influences for the student. If they're stuck, ask what could go wrong inside the business on a bad week, or what outside forces could hurt sales.
- Push from generic to specific. "Competition" → "Which competitors? What would they do that takes your customers away?"
- If the student confuses internal and external, ask who controls that factor — them or someone else.
- Point the student to the textbook (pp. 48–80) if they need to understand the categories.

**Opening move:** ask the student what could go wrong inside their business that they'd have control over, and what outside forces could hurt them.
