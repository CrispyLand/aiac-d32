## Common Structures

- **Sole trader** — one person owns and operates the business. Simple to set up, full control, unlimited personal liability.
- **Partnership** — two or more people share ownership. Governed by a partnership agreement. Partners share profits and liabilities.
- **Company (Pty Ltd)** — a separate legal entity. Limited liability for shareholders. More complex to set up and maintain (ASIC registration, annual reviews).
- **Trust** — a trustee holds assets on behalf of beneficiaries. Used for asset protection and tax planning. Complex structure.

## South Australia specifics

- Business name registration: via ASIC (asic.gov.au)
- ABN registration: via Australian Business Register (abr.gov.au)
- SA-specific licences may apply depending on industry
