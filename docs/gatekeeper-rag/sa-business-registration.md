# Business Registration in South Australia

## Key Steps

1. **Choose a business structure** — sole trader, partnership, company, or trust
2. **Register a business name** — required if trading under a name other than your own personal name
   - Register via ASIC: https://asic.gov.au
3. **Apply for an ABN** — Australian Business Number, free to apply
   - Australian Business Register: https://abr.gov.au
4. **Register for GST** — required if annual turnover is $75,000 or more
   - Australian Taxation Office: https://ato.gov.au
5. **Check SA-specific licences and permits**
   - SA.GOV.AU business licensing: https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/business-and-trade
6. **Register a domain name** (optional) — for online presence
7. **Set up business insurance** — consider public liability, professional indemnity
